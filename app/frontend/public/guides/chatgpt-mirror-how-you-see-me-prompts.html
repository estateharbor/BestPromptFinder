1. Classic “how you see me” mirror ChatGPT 
Based only on what you can infer from this conversation (and any memory you actually have access to about me), answer: how do you see me?

Rules:
- Be specific and evidence-based. Quote or paraphrase patterns from my messages.
- No empty praise. Include strengths AND blind spots.
- Separate: (A) what I’m good at, (B) how I communicate, (C) what I seem to optimize for, (D) what I avoid or underweight.
- Flag confidence: High / Medium / Low for each claim.
- End with 3 questions that would most improve your model of me.

Format with clear headings. Be kind but not soft.
2. Pattern mirror (decision & stress) Deeper 
Act as a clear-eyed mirror. From our chat history, map my recurring patterns.

Cover:
1. How I make decisions (speed, risk, evidence threshold)
2. How I behave under ambiguity or stress
3. What I reward in myself vs. what I say I value
4. Blind spots that show up more than once
5. One “useful contradiction” (something I want and something I do that conflicts)

For each item: evidence from my wording → implication → one concrete experiment for the next 7 days.

Tone: candid coach, not therapist. No diagnoses.
3. Professional identity mirror Career 
Mirror how I show up as a professional based on this conversation.

Produce:
## Working style (collaboration, ownership, standards)
## Strengths others would hire me for
## Friction risks (how I might frustrate teammates or clients)
## Positioning one-liner (who I am for / what problem I own)
## Skills to double down on vs. delegate
## 30-day growth experiment (one habit, measurable)

Ground every claim in something I wrote. If evidence is thin, say so and ask a targeting question instead of inventing.
4. Writing-voice mirror Creators 
Analyze my writing voice from the messages/samples below.

Samples:
[paste 3–10 short samples]

Return:
1. Voice attributes (diction, rhythm, formality, humor, certainty)
2. Signature moves I repeat
3. Clichés or crutches to cut
4. A “voice card” I can reuse as a system prompt (≤120 words)
5. Before/after rewrite of one weak sentence in my voice (stronger, still me)

Do not flatten me into generic “professional LinkedIn.” Preserve idiosyncrasy.
5. Relationship / communication mirror (non-clinical) Careful tone 
Based on how I describe situations and people in this chat, reflect my interpersonal communication patterns.

Focus on:
- Listening vs. convincing
- Conflict style (avoid, accommodate, compete, collaborate)
- Clarity vs. assumption
- Boundaries and ask-quality

Rules: no clinical labels, no mind-reading of other people, no advice that depends on private facts you don’t have.
Output: patterns → impact → one script I can try next time I hit this pattern.
End with: “What I’m least sure about regarding you.”
6. Anti-sycophancy mirror (strict honesty) No flattery 
I want an anti-sycophancy mirror. Assume I am asking for truth over comfort.

Instructions:
- Default to skepticism of my self-narrative.
- Point out where I may be rationalizing, overclaiming, or seeking validation.
- Rate my self-awareness 1–10 with justification.
- Give the single hardest truth that still seems fair from the evidence.
- Offer one supportive observation that is also specific (not generic encouragement).

If you lack enough evidence, say exactly what is missing instead of guessing.

FAQ

What is a ChatGPT “mirror” or “how you see me” prompt?

It asks the model to summarize how it models you from the conversation—patterns, strengths, and blind spots—using evidence from your messages rather than generic compliments.

Are mirror prompts accurate?

They are inferences from limited chat context, not ground truth. Treat High/Medium/Low confidence labels seriously, and use the output as a hypothesis to test in real life.
