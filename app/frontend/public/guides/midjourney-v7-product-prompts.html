FAQ

How should I structure a Midjourney v7 product prompt?

Lead with the product and materials, then lighting, lens/camera, composition, mood, and end with parameters like --ar, --stylize, and --v 7. Keep material wording consistent across a shot family.

What --stylize values work for product shots?

For catalog accuracy, lower stylize (about 50–120) usually keeps materials honest. Push higher only when you want a more illustrative campaign look.

How do I get more Midjourney prompts ranked for my SKU?
